SEEDING BRIEF: quick_sync
=========================
Read with the `write-seed-segments` skill, which holds the shared labelling rules. This brief
only adds what is specific to quick syncs.

THE MEETING
- 2–3 people, 150–300 words in total, often unscheduled ("got five minutes?").
- One narrow topic: unblock a task, agree a next step, hand something over.
- Very little ceremony. Context is one or two lines; closing is a quick "cool, thanks".
- Dense in action items: most quick syncs end with at least one owner + deadline.

REGISTER
- Informal, short turns, first names, contractions, half-sentences ("Yep.", "Makes sense.").
- People talk about the work directly; little background is explained because both sides know it.

WHAT EACH ZONE LOOKS LIKE HERE
- context (S1 opens): why we're talking. "Quick one about the {CLIENT} renewal before I send it."
  The purpose sentence is importance 1 only if it states the topic in a notes-worthy way;
  greetings and "got a sec?" are 0.
- discussion: the one problem and a fact or two. Important (1): the blocker, the number, the
  constraint ("Legal needs the signed form before {DATE_REL}."). Chatter (0): "Ugh, again?",
  "Right, I remember that."
- decision: small, concrete choices. "Let's just use the old template for now." (1). Agreement
  lines after it ("Fine by me.") are decision, 0.
- action_item: the heart of a quick sync. "{S2|owner} will ping {PERSON} today." (1). Often two
  action items, one per person. Confirmations ("Great.") are action_item, 0.
- closing: one or two lines. "Okay, that's all I needed." (0). A follow-up time ("Let's check
  back {DATE_REL}.") is 1.

GOOD SEGMENT SHAPES
- 2–5 sentences. Quick syncs rarely need 8+ sentences in one segment.
- Action segments where the owner volunteers ("I can take that") and the requester confirms.
- An action item that is negotiated: first deadline rejected, second one accepted (only the
  final commitment is 1).

AVOID
- Long status reports (that's a standup) or formal decision language (that's a review).
- Every segment ending in "Thanks!": vary the last line.

EXAMPLE (action_item, quick_sync, customer_support)

{"speaker": "S2", "text": "I've got context on that one.", "importance": 0, "zone": "action_item"}
{"speaker": "S2", "text": "{S2|owner} will answer it by {DATE_REL|deadline}.", "importance": 1, "zone": "action_item"}
{"speaker": "S1", "text": "Perfect, thank you.", "importance": 0, "zone": "action_item"}
